Questions about Solo In Joburg

> Who is this book for?

Anyone starting over in a new city, living on their own, or curious about what it takes to build a new life from scratch. You don’t need to know Johannesburg to relate to this story.

> Is this a memoir or a travel guide?

Both. It is Shaun’s personal account of 14 months building a new life in the Joburg CBD, and it includes the practical realities of finding a home, managing money, dating and handling obstacles and setbacks.

About Shaun Mahabeer

Shaun Mahabeer is a South African author, media personality and multidisciplinary creative based in Johannesburg. Through his writing, art, food and digital media, he explores what it means to live independently, find peace and experience the world on your own terms.

He is the author of Solo In Joburg, Solo Journeys, Shaun Eats and Peace and How To Have It. His work spans memoir, food, personal exploration and everyday life, bringing together a curiosity about the world and a desire to live with greater freedom, creativity and intention.

Based in Johannesburg, Shaun shares his experiences and perspectives through books, podcasting and social media, documenting the places he explores, the food he makes and the ideas that shape how he lives.
