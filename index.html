Solo In Joburg is a memoir about Shaun building a new life in Johannesburg's CBD over 14 months, on his own.

He shares the practical realities of finding a home, managing money, navigating dating, and becoming independent—including the challenge of living through an eight-month water outage.

Part personal account, part guide to life in the city, this is the story of what it took to make a new city home.
